---
title: Rollouts pay Griselbrand's 7 life until they die, so stripping the ability reads as a gain
discoveredBy: 8271
status: draft
confidence: high
---

**What is wrong.** With Griselbrand on the Bot's side, rollouts activate "pay 7 life: draw seven" often enough to lose the game to it. Any effect that removes the ability therefore raises mean reward.

**Evidence.** Oko on the Bot's own Griselbrand, no opposing board: the +1 scored mean reward 0.750 against 0.642 for passing, on all 5 seeds, while its margin was lower (601 against 694). The same position with a vanilla Craw Wurm does not elk it. Recurring Nightmare swapping Griselbrand for Grizzly Bears (4/5 seeds) and Teferi bouncing it behave alike.

**Why it matters.** The level-2 entries for Oko, Teferi and Recurring Nightmare use a Craw Wurm to stay out of this, so nothing pins it. A pay-life ability in a rollout's default policy is the likely class, wider than this card.
