**What is wrong.** Roughly 110 comments across the engine attach the claim
"omitted duration means the effect is INDEFINITE / lasts until the end of the
game" to **CR 611.2b**. Printed, 611.2b is the _"for as long as …"_ rule (Master
Thief); the sentence those comments paraphrase — "If no duration is stated, it
lasts until the end of the game" — is the second half of **CR 611.2a**.

**Evidence.**

```
$ bun run cr 611.2a
611.2a A continuous effect generated by the resolution of a spell or ability
lasts as long as stated by the spell or ability creating it (such as "until end
of turn"). If no duration is stated, it lasts until the end of the game.

$ bun run cr 611.2b
611.2b Some continuous effects generated by the resolution of a spell or ability
have durations worded "for as long as . . . ."
```

Representative sites (issue #3459 corrected only its own nine new ones):
`convex/gre/state.ts:851` (the `animation` record's `duration` field),
`convex/gre/state.ts:12470` / `:12795` (`revertAnimation`, CR 400.7 end-of-life),
`convex/cards/types.ts` (`AnimateSpec.duration`'s own doc),
`convex/cards/mechanicsRegistry.ts`'s `animate` row.

`bun run cr:lint` cannot catch it: the id RESOLVES, and the second scan only
checks that a `CR 701.N`/`702.N` citation names its own section title. This is
exactly the blind spot CLAUDE.md records — "a resolvable but wrong id outside
701/702".

**Why it may not deserve its own issue.** It is a comment-only correction with no
behavioural consequence, and the two subrules are adjacent, so nothing was
implemented wrong because of it. It is worth one mechanical sweep (the phrase
"INDEFINITE" or "no duration" on the same line as `CR 611.2b` finds nearly all of
them) rather than a ticket of its own — or a line on whichever CR-hygiene tracker
is open.
