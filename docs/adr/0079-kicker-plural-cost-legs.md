# Kicker becomes plural and reuses the alternative-cost leg vocabulary (`CostLegs`)

Status: accepted

## Context

Planeshift (PLS, PRD #1935) needs two things the shipped Kicker cannot express, and both are load-bearing for 12 of its 143 cards:

1. **Non-mana kicker costs.** `KickerCost.cost` is typed `ManaCost`. PLS kicks with sacrifices (Bog Down, Magma Burst — two lands; Primal Growth — a creature; Falling Timber, Pollen Remedy, Rushing River — a land), a bounce (Arctic Merfolk — "return a creature you control"), life (Phyrexian Scuta — "pay 3 life") and a mana-plus-discard (Dralnu's Pet — `{2}{B}` and discard a creature card). CR 702.33a says a kicker cost is an additional cost of **any** kind; the engine had only ever met mana ones.
2. **Two independently payable kickers on one card.** The Battlemage cycle (Nightscape / Stormscape / Sunscape / Thornscape / Thunderscape) reads "Kicker {A} and/or {B}", with one intervening-if ETB trigger per kicker: _"if it was kicked with its {2}{U} kicker, …"_. `CardDefinition.kicker` is singular and the payment record is `StackItem.kickerCount: number` — a count can say _how many times_ a kicker was paid but never _which_ of two was paid, and both may be paid, firing both triggers.

Kicker is not a greenfield seam. **25 shipped cards** declare it (INV ×20 plus zen, znr, wwk, dmu, eoe), and `kickerCount` is read from `convex/game.ts`, `convex/gre/state.ts`, `entersWith.ts` (`count: "kicker"` counters), the Effect Script value `{ kickerCount: true }`, `serialize.ts`, the bot's `candidateValue`/`grounding`, and — client-side — `src/hooks/useHandCardCommit.tsx` and `src/components/cards/cast-cost-dialog.tsx`. Any reshape is a cross-layer migration, which is why it is sequenced as the PLS rollout's **first** slice, as a framework-only ticket shipping zero cards.

Meanwhile `AlternativeCost` (CR 118.9) already carries exactly the leg vocabulary the new kicker costs need — a mana leg, a permanent leg (`action: "return" | "sacrifice"` × `count` × `filter`), a life leg (`payLife`) and a hand leg (`handCost: { action: "exile" | "discard", requirements }`) — and is already reused verbatim by evoke, dash, flashback and madness.

## Decision

**Extract the legs, make kicker plural, and key payment by kicker id.**

- **`CostLegs` is extracted from `AlternativeCost`** — the mana / permanent / life / hand leg fields, with no `id`, `description` or `condition`. Then `AlternativeCost = CostLegs & { id, description, condition? }` and `KickerCost = CostLegs & { id, description, multi? }`. The leg-level helpers in `convex/gre/alternativeCost.ts` (`matchingPermanentsForAltCost`, `altCostHasPermanentLeg`, `matchingHandCardsForAltCost`, `canPayHandCost`, `buildAlternativeCostChoice`, `buildAlternativeCostHandChoice`, `validateAlternativeHandCostPicks`) retype from `AlternativeCost` to `CostLegs`. Zero behaviour change for alternative costs — this is a widening, and the leg helpers were already cost-agnostic.
- **`MayPayCost` collapses onto `CostLegs` too, in a PRELIMINARY slice that lands before this one** (tracked-by: #1933). The repo carried three overlapping cost-leg vocabularies — `AlternativeCost`, `MayPayCost` (mana / `sacrifice:{filter,count|{minTotalPower}}` / `life` / `discard:{count}`) and the `additionalCosts` bag — and they had already drifted in **both** directions: `MayPayCost.discard` has no filter where `CostLegs.handCost` does, and `CostLegs` had no `minTotalPower` where `MayPayCost.sacrifice` does. Unifying the two genuine leg vocabularies makes the union the single authority and hands the Planeshift Lair lands their return-to-hand leg for free instead of adding the same leg twice. `CostLegs` therefore takes the **nested** leg shape (`permanent?: { action, filter, count }`, `hand?: { action, requirements }`) rather than `AlternativeCost`'s sibling `action`/`count`/`filter` trio, so an orphan `count` with no `filter` is unrepresentable. Migration is far smaller than the raw consumer count suggests: of 75 `mayPay` declarations only **9** carry a non-mana leg, plus **7** `AlternativeCost` permanent legs and 7 `entersTappedUnlessPay` sites — ~16 declarations, not 71. `MayPayCost` keeps its bare-`ManaCost` shorthand via the existing `normalizeMayPayCost`.
- **`additionalCosts` stays out.** Unlike the other two it is not purely a cost vocabulary: `xFromOpponentGraveyard` is a COMPUTED value that is never paid, `payXLife` is a caster-CHOSEN amount, and `flashbackExileFromGraveyard` is a keyword-conditional graveyard leg whose picker is already shared with escape and delve. Folding non-costs into a cost type would buy uniformity by lying about what those members are. Tracked as its own follow-up.
- **Only the LEGS are shared, never the SELECTION.** A kicker is an _additional_ cost (CR 601.2f / 702.33a) added on top of the printed cost; an alternative cost _replaces_ it (CR 118.9). So `getAlternativeCost`, `affordableAlternativeCosts`, `canPayAlternativeCost` and `alternativeCostConditionMet` are **not** on kicker's path, and kicker gets no `condition` field. Routing kicker through the alt-cost selector would make paying a kicker mutually exclusive with paying the card's real cost — precisely backwards.
- **`kicker?: KickerCost` → `kickers?: KickerCost[]`.** One field, not a legacy singular beside a new plural: two fields for one mechanic is the drift the single-authority rule exists to prevent. The 25 shipped declarations migrate mechanically to a one-element array with `id: "kicker"`.
- **`StackItem.kickerCount: number` → `kickerPayments: Record<KickerId, number>`, with `kickerCount` retained as a DERIVED sum.** Every existing consumer that only wants a total (`{ kickerCount: true }`, `count: "kicker"`, the bot valuers) keeps working unchanged; `multi: true` (CR 702.33e, Everflowing Chalice) becomes per-kicker rather than per-card. A new frozen Effect Script value `{ kickerPaid: "<id>" }` answers the Battlemages' per-kicker intervening-ifs.
- **Non-mana kicker legs are paid as a Model-2 pre-payment pending choice** on `PendingCast`, following the delve/convoke precedent of ADR 0063 exactly: choose which kicker ids to pay → each paid kicker's non-mana legs open their pickers → mana auto-taps the remainder. Which permanent is sacrificed or returned is **always the caster's explicit choice**, routed through the unified `sacrificeChoice` layer — never auto-picked, even when only one permanent is legal, because a forced pick is still information the player must see.

## Considered options

- **Extend `additionalCosts`.** Rejected. It is a flat bag of one-off optional fields (`sacrificeFilter`, `exileFilter`, `payLife`, `payXLife`, `xFromOpponentGraveyard`, `flashbackExileFromGraveyard`) with "provide exactly one of" constraints, no id, and no composition. Kicker needs _two independently payable_ costs each with _multiple legs_ — the bag cannot express that, and growing it a seventh time would deepen the wrong shape.
- **Leave `MayPayCost` as a separate third vocabulary and just add a `return` leg to it.** Considered and rejected once the migration was actually sized. The case for it rested on a consumer count of "71 declarations across 46 card files", which turned out to count every file merely _mentioning_ `mayPay` — overwhelmingly mana-only costs a leg reshape never touches. The true figure is ~16 declarations. At that price, permanently keeping two vocabularies that had already drifted in both directions, and writing the return leg twice, is the worse trade.
- **A new bespoke kicker cost type.** Rejected as card-shaped duplication of a leg vocabulary that already exists and is already proven by four keywords (primitive-reuse rule): the legs PLS needs are, field for field, the legs `AlternativeCost` already has.
- **Keep `kicker?` and add `kickers?` alongside.** Rejected — two authorities for one mechanic, and every consumer would need to read both forever.
- **Keep `kickerCount: number` and add a parallel paid-ids set.** Rejected for the same reason: the count would become derivable from the set yet independently writable, so the two can drift. Making `kickerCount` a derived read of `kickerPayments` gives back-compat with one source of truth.

## Consequences

- `kickerPayments` is a new optional `GameState`/stack-item field → it must be registered in `PERSISTED_OPTIONAL_KEYS` (`serialize.ts`) with a round-trip smoke test, or the schema drift guard fails.
- The non-mana kicker leg pickers are new pending-choice shapes → each needs a compile-time-exhaustive `botActionRealisation` branch, or the bot **stalls** casting Bog Down. This is the recurring bot-driver failure mode; the dispatch must stay exhaustive by construction, not by a hand-maintained kind list.
- Frontend is not optional: `cast-cost-dialog.tsx` must offer N independent kicker toggles instead of one, `useHandCardCommit.tsx` must carry the chosen kicker ids, and the new cost shape must be added to the affordability catalogue sweep (`src/lib/__tests__/activation-affordability.catalogue.test.ts`) and gated in `getStackAbilities`. Every SURFACE assertion runs through the real view reducers.
- Full 5-layer integration coverage is mandatory (GRE → `game.ts` mutation → wire projection → cast-cost UI → bot), since the migration touches all five. The 25 shipped kicker cards are the regression suite: their behaviour must be provably unchanged.
- Mechanics Registry rows for **Kicker** and **Multikicker** stay `implemented`; their notes gain the plural/`CostLegs` shape and the `{ kickerPaid }` value.
- After this lands, PLS's 12 kicker cards are ordinary free-tranche cards written as Effect Scripts inside their colour modules — no stubs, no second touch of any colour file. That is the whole reason the slice is sequenced first.
