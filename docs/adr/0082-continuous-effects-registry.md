# One Continuous Effects Registry as the source of truth for characteristic-changing effects (CR 611/613)

## Status

proposed

## Context

Issue #1329 asks for one small thing: Pledge of Loyalty grants the enchanted
creature "protection from the colors of permanents you control" — a protection
_set_ that must be re-read as the board changes. Pulling that thread exposed
that layer 6 cannot express it at all, for a structural reason, and that the
reason generalises to every layer.

The engine today runs **two incompatible models** for continuous effects:

| Layer                     | Model                                                                               | Where                                                         |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 7 (P/T)                   | **Recompute at every read** — `getStaticPTBuff` walks every battlefield source live | `gre/layers.ts:162`, `getEffectivePower/Toughness` `:459-469` |
| 6 (abilities)             | **Materialise once** — the keyword string is pushed into `staticAbilities[]`        | `applySourceStaticEffects` `gre/state.ts:5840-5938`           |
| 2–5 (control/type/colour) | mixed helpers, no single authority                                                  | `gre/layers.ts:80-153`, `gre/state.ts`                        |

Layer 7 is CR-shaped: CR 613.1 says characteristics are recomputed _every time
they are checked_, and nothing is cached. Layer 6 is not. Its model is
materialise-at-attach, with one narrow re-evaluation path
(`refreshCounterGatedStatics`, `state.ts:6467`) that re-runs `applies`/`condition`
only for counter-gated sources on SBA passes. The code says so itself
(`state.ts:6429-6440`): "Unlike `pt-buff`/`pt-cda` — recomputed at every read —
nothing re-runs [keyword-grant] afterwards", and lists the staleness bugs that
follow.

The decisive constraint is that **a grant's parameter is fixed at
materialisation time**. `StaticKeywordGrant.keyword` is a `string`
(`cards/types.ts:5245-5276`), pushed verbatim. Protection is then recovered by
_regex-parsing that string back out_ at every check
(`PROTECTION_FROM_COLOR_REGEX`, `gre/protection.ts:29`). There is no mechanism
by which the parameter of a keyword — a colour set, a landwalk subtype, a
rampage N — is recomputed. Adding one to the materialise model means inventing
a second, parallel notion of "live-ness" alongside the existing one.

A narrower fix was considered first and rejected on CR grounds: a
`dynamicKeyword: (source, ctx) => string` closure evaluated at the point the
keyword is _consulted_. It works for Pledge of Loyalty, and it leaves the
existing CR 613.7 timestamp machinery (`grantedStaticAbilities` /
`removedKeywords` / `abilitiesSuppressedBy`, all on one monotonic `staticSeq`,
hardened by issue #1715 against Gravity-Sphere-then-Flight and
Humility-then-Fire-Whip) completely untouched. But it entrenches the divergence
rather than closing it: two live-ness mechanisms, and layer 6 still not
recomputed per read.

Committing to per-read recompute for layer 6 then surfaced the real blocker.
`staticAbilities` receives grants from **three provenances**, only one of which
is derivable from the live board:

| Provenance                          | Example                                    | Live source to walk?                 |
| ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------ |
| Static ability of a permanent       | Crusade, an aura                           | yes                                  |
| `grantAbility` Op with a `duration` | Touch of Vitae (haste EOT), Dash, Backup   | **no** — the spell resolved and left |
| Counter-keyed                       | `applyKeywordCounterGrant` `state.ts:5752` | from the counter, not a source       |

`removedKeywords` has the same split (`state.ts:491` — a source-keyed continuous
removal _and_ a duration-scoped one). So "walk the battlefield and recompute"
is not expressible: the duration-scoped effects are residue of a resolved spell
(CR 611.2b) with no permanent to walk.

The CR does not have this problem, because the CR does not make this
distinction. CR 611 knows only _continuous effects_, each with a timestamp;
whether one was generated by a static ability of a permanent or left behind by
a resolved spell affects its duration, not its identity or its ordering. The
engine's split into "static effects of sources" plus "materialised residue on
instances" is an implementation artefact with no CR counterpart, and it is what
makes a uniform recompute impossible.

## Decision

**One `continuousEffects` registry on `GameState` is the single source of truth
for every characteristic-changing continuous effect.** Each entry carries its
layer, its timestamp, its expiry, and what it does. Effects from a permanent's
static abilities, effects generated for a duration by a resolved spell or
ability, and effects borne by counters are all entries in that one list,
distinguished only by how they expire.

Characteristics are then **derived at read time from the registry alone**, for
all layers. No materialised copy on a `CardInstanceState` is authoritative.

Five decisions bound the scope:

1. **All CR 613 layers, in one move — not layer 6 only.** Layer 7 already
   recomputes per read, so a layer-6-only registry would leave two models in
   place indefinitely, which is the condition this ADR exists to end. The
   registry subsumes `getStaticPTBuff` and the layer 2–5 helpers too.

2. **Characteristic-changing effects only (CR 613). Rules-modifying effects
   (CR 611.3) stay out.** `attack-restriction`, `block-requirement`,
   `cost-modifier`, `cast-restriction`, `cast-timing-lock`, `untap-restriction`,
   `hand-size-override`, `permanent-guard`, `mana-substitution`,
   `landwalk-negation`, `enters-tapped-restriction` change no characteristic and
   the CR deliberately keeps them out of the layer system. They live in the same
   `StaticEffect` union today, which is the incoherence; the registry boundary is
   drawn where the CR draws it, not where the union does. This roughly halves the
   surface.

3. **Ordering is layer + timestamp (CR 613.7). CR 613.8 dependency is a
   documented divergence, not implemented.** It is not implemented today either
   — `types.ts:6542` notes a case avoided by construction rather than handled —
   so this preserves current fidelity rather than reducing it, and turns an
   implicit hole into a tracked one. Dependency detection requires evaluating
   each effect twice to see whether another changes its outcome, plus loop
   handling (CR 613.8b); coupling that graph problem to this rework would risk
   both. The registry is, however, the only structure in which 613.8 becomes
   implementable at all, since it is the first place every effect is visible
   together.

4. **The wire keeps a materialised snapshot; the client does not re-derive.**
   `projectPublicState` writes the derived values into the fields the client
   already reads. 53 client call sites (`useBattlefieldVisualState`,
   `lib/banding.ts`, `damage-assignment-panel`, `summoning-sickness-badge`, …)
   are unchanged. The field stops being authoritative input and becomes derived
   output. This is not a fidelity compromise: the client re-renders on every
   state push, so its snapshot cannot go stale, and per ADR 0074 the client
   never holds authority regardless.

5. **PRD-scale, sequenced apart from its trigger.** This is a core-engine
   rework — 202 server call sites read `.staticAbilities` directly, 295
   `staticEffects` declarations across 134 card files, and the timestamp
   machinery it touches has its own regression history (#1715). It becomes its
   own PRD with its own slices. Issue #1329 stays the INV capability slice and
   becomes a consumer: Pledge of Loyalty declares a dependency on this PRD and
   waits, while Winnow and Pure Reflection (which need only DSL additions —
   a `$target0` reserved ref and an `EffectValue`-valued token P/T) are
   independent of it and ship first.

## Consequences

- Two models become one. The staleness class documented at `state.ts:6429-6440`
  is closed by construction rather than by widening a refresh path.
- A keyword's _parameter_ becomes recomputable, which is what #1329 needed.
  Protection can stop being a regex round-trip through a string
  (`protection.ts:29`) and become a set derived per read. Dynamic landwalk
  subtypes and rampage N get the same for free.
- `serialize.ts` is affected: `continuousEffects` is a new `GameState` key and
  must be classified into `PERSISTED_OPTIONAL_KEYS` or `TRANSIENT_KEYS`, with a
  round-trip smoke test — the drift guard fails otherwise.
- Per-read derivation must not become a per-read battlefield sweep on hot paths
  (SBA, combat, the client-side Brain's search). Registry reads need to be cheap
  or memoised within a resolution step; this is the main performance risk and
  should be measured, not assumed, against the bot suite's per-test ceiling.
- CR 613.8 remains unimplemented, tracked by **#2068**, which any
  `// divergence` marker must reference per the documented-divergence guard
  (`.claude/rules/gre-development.md`, Guard B).
- Rules-modifying effects (CR 611.3) keep their current, separate handling.
  A future ADR may unify their duration/expiry handling with the registry's
  without absorbing them into the layer system.
